patches: back the events table up before applying the event_type guard

001_event_type_guard.sql used to refuse while any row violated the CHECK,
which left it permanently blocked by the smoke-test row. It now copies
public.events into a timestamped table in a new `archive` schema, then
removes only the offending rows from the live table, both inside one
transaction. Nothing is destroyed, and `archive` is not in PGRST_DB_SCHEMAS
so the copy is unreachable over the API.

Verified against a scratch restore of kankyouken-20260909T030001.sql.gz:
9 rows archived, 1 removed (test_event), constraint added, and an insert
carrying a typo'd event_type rejected.

Also adds scripts/probe-enrolment.sh, which checks that one enrolment
actually landed — participant row, one consent record per study, and any
client_event_id delivered more than once — and drops the Impressum and
Datenschutz placeholders from www/index.html.
